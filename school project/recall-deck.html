How does ATP synthase generate ATP during oxidative phosphorylation?

Hint: Think of proton gradients and mechanical rotary action.
Solution Click card to turn back

Protons move down their electrochemical gradient from the intermembrane space into the mitochondrial matrix through the F0 subunit of ATP synthase, causing it to rotate. This rotational energy drives the catalytic F1 subunit to phosphorylate ADP with inorganic phosphate into ATP.
